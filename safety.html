Mountain Safety

Explore Safely, Travel Confidently

Good preparation makes every mountain journey safer, easier and more enjoyable.

Safety Guide

Be Prepared

Safety Starts Before the Journey

The mountains reward those who prepare. Weather can change quickly, trails can be demanding and help is not always nearby. A little planning before you leave home is what turns a risky trip into a safe and memorable one.

  • Check the weather
  • Plan your route
  • Carry enough water
  • Take essential equipment

Know Before You Go

Essential Mountain Safety Tips

Check the Weather

Always check weather conditions before starting your journey.

Plan Your Route

Know your route and stay on marked paths.

Carry Proper Gear

Take suitable shoes, clothing, water and essential equipment.

Stay Connected

Keep your phone charged and inform someone about your travel plan.

Follow Local Guidance

Listen to local guides and follow mountain rules.

Respect Nature

Do not damage plants, disturb wildlife or leave waste behind.
